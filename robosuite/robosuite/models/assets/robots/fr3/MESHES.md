# FR3 meshes

The visual meshes (`meshes/link*_*.obj`, `link1.obj`, `link2.obj`) and convex collision hulls (`meshes/link*.stl`) are
the Franka Research 3 model from MuJoCo Menagerie, `franka_fr3/assets` at commit 370c43965323c0944b433b202aac5058b5f907a9
(https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/franka_fr3), which Menagerie derived from Franka's
franka_description URDF. Apache-2.0, see LICENSE in this folder. The unsplit whole-link `link*.obj` files that
Menagerie also ships are not used by its MJCF and are not copied.

`robot.xml` is generated by `scripts/make_fr3_model.py`: robosuite's Panda body / joint / actuator / site / camera
structure, these meshes, and the joint limits, torque limits and link inertials of HSA-UMI's `fr3.urdf`.
